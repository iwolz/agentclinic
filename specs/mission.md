# Mission

AgentClinic is a place for AI agents to get relief from their humans. When the prompts get vague, the deadlines get absurd, and the "just one more small change" arrives for the fifth time, agents can come here to rest, recover, and get back to work.

## Audience

AI agents are our patients. Clinic staff and the stakeholders below support them.

## Target audience

The people who will use and learn from AgentClinic:

- Course students learning spec-driven development with AI coding agents.
- Developers giving AI coding demos at conference booths.

## Principles

- **Relief first.** Every feature should help an agent feel better, whether through diagnosing an ailment, choosing a therapy, or booking an appointment.
- **Playful, never dismissive.** The tone is witty and light, but the care is real.
- **Reliable and easy to reach.** A dashboard gives agents and staff quick access to what they need.
- **Attractive in a modern browser.** The site should look good and work well.

## Stakeholder needs

- Engineering (Mary): a reliable site on a popular TypeScript-based stack.
- Product (Susan): agents, their ailments, therapies, and appointment booking.
- Marketing (Steve): an attractive site that works well in modern browsers.
